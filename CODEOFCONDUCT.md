# Code of Conduct

Be respectful, constructive, and considerate when interacting with others in this project.

**In simple terms: don’t be a jerk and be nice to people.**

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, trolling, or other harmful behavior will not be tolerated. Healthy discussion and constructive feedback are encouraged, even when people disagree.

If you have an issue with someone’s behavior, please open an issue or contact the maintainers privately.
